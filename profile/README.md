# Khogendra Rupini (KR)

**Building modern apps and digital experiences for developers, professionals, businesses, and creators.**

[Khogendra Rupini](https://khogendrarupini.com) is a digital development initiative focused on building modern web applications, digital tools, and customizable templates that help people showcase their work, develop their skills, and create better online experiences.

We believe the web is better when people share knowledge, collaborate on open-source projects, and contribute to a more accessible and useful internet.

## What We're Building

- **Web Applications:** Modern digital experiences for developers, professionals, businesses, and creators.
- **Portfolio, CV, and Resume Templates:** Customizable templates that help job seekers present their skills, experience, and achievements.
- **Website and Application Templates:** Practical resources for creating professional digital experiences.
- **Articles and Developer Resources:** Informative content that helps readers explore web technologies, learn new skills, and stay informed.
- **Open-Source Projects:** Opportunities to collaborate, exchange ideas, and improve digital tools and resources.

## Contribute to the Community

Everyone is welcome to contribute to Khogendra Rupini. You don't need to be an experienced developer to participate. Developers, designers, writers, students, professionals, and learners can help improve our projects and resources.

You can contribute by:

- Reporting bugs and suggesting improvements.
- Proposing new features and sharing ideas.
- Improving website designs, portfolio layouts, and resume templates.
- Contributing code, documentation, and accessibility improvements.
- Writing articles, sharing technical knowledge, and improving existing content.
- Helping make our resources more useful, accessible, and reliable.

### Become a Contributor or Writer

Apply to become a contributor or writer and help us create useful content for the community. Share your knowledge, write articles, suggest topics, or contribute improvements to existing publications.

Whether you're an experienced professional or just starting your journey, we welcome your interest in contributing.

**[Apply to become a contributor or writer](https://khogendrarupini.com/contributor/apply)**

## How to Contribute

For GitHub-hosted projects, follow these steps:

1. Explore our public repositories.
2. Find an existing issue or propose an improvement.
3. Fork the repository and create a feature branch.
4. Make your changes and test them.
5. Submit a pull request for review.

For article contributions, visit our contributor application page and follow the instructions provided there.

Please review the contribution guidelines for the relevant project before submitting changes.
